Services

Every quote lists the prep as well as the paint, because prep is where a finish is won or lost.

Interior

Living spaces, bedrooms, hallways and stairwells. We move and cover furniture, protect floors, and seal off the room so dust stays put.

Includes
Walls, ceilings, trim and doors
Prep
Patching, sanding, caulking and primer where needed
Colour
A preview of your chosen colours before we start

Exterior

Siding, trim, decks and fences. We wash and scrape first, then paint in dry weather, so the finish lasts through a few winters.

Includes
Siding, soffits, trim, doors, decks and fences
Prep
Washing, scraping, sanding and spot priming
Timing
Booked around the weather, with a rain plan

Cabinets

Kitchen cabinets and bathroom vanities, refinished instead of replaced. Doors come off and are numbered so they go back where they belong.

Includes
Doors, drawer fronts and frames
Prep
Degreasing, sanding and a bonding primer
Finish
A hard-wearing enamel, brushed or sprayed

Not part of a standard quote

Wallpaper removal, drywall replacement and anything structural are quoted separately, and we'll say so at the visit.
