The small things that make a site look unfinished

I went through more than 20 Ontario small-business sites built on free website builders. All seven of these problems turned up. Each one is quick to fix.

  1. 1

    The browser tab says “Mysite”

    The tab shows your business name and what you do

  2. 2

    The address ends in wixsite.com or weebly.com

    Your own .ca or .com address, in your name

  3. 3

    A banner advertises the website builder

    Nothing on the page but your business

  4. 4

    Every menu link goes to the same page

    Each page has its own address, so Google can find it

  5. 5

    The phone number is a placeholder like 123-456-7890

    Your real number, one tap to call

  6. 6

    Social icons open the builder’s accounts, not yours

    Links go to your Facebook and Instagram

  7. 7

    The footer says “© 2023 by The Painting Company”

    Your business name and the current year
